\note{Paragraph 3: what this paper does. A standard two-strain model with an effector response, simulated exactly \citep{gillespie1977exact,gillespie2007stochastic} and compared with its deterministic limit; three phenomena each with an analytical check; the chronic set point $I^* = d_E/q$ as the organising quantity; the deterministic predictions that fail and when they hold; full reproducibility.}
