\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What the deterministic model gets wrong, and when it is fine}
\note{Large populations: fine. Small numbers at three moments. The set point $I^* = d_E/q$ as the single diagnostic.}

\subsection{Relation to previous work}
\note{Branching theory of early infection \citep{pearson2011stochastic}; immune activation as a birth--death process (new framing, same mathematics as \citet{kendall1948generalized}); pre-existing versus de novo resistance \citep{alexander2012preexistence}; system-size dependence.}

\subsection{Limitations}
\note{Illustrative parameters, not fitted; one mutant, no back mutation; no effector source term (so collapse is permanent); no latent reservoir; no pharmacokinetics; horizons (day 200 map, day 120 therapy grid); lineage theories a few percent low where realised loads outgrow the deterministic path.}

\subsection{On the provenance of this study}
\note{Grew from a course project whose deterministic conclusions did not survive exact simulation; the corrections are part of the result. Keep generic in the anonymised build.}
